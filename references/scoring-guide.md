# Scoring Rubric — UN Jobs Search

## 3-Dimension Formula (100 max)

TOTAL MATCH (%) = (Technical Relevance × 0.60) + (Seniority Alignment × 0.20) + (Strategic Alignment × 0.20)

---

### 1. Technical Relevance (60%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Domain expertise match (e.g., Digital Education, AI in Learning, EdTech)
- Relevance of past experience to job responsibilities
- Hands-on vs theoretical experience
- Tools, technologies, and methodologies
- Sector alignment (education, public sector, development)

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 90-100 | Direct and deep expertise |
| 75-89 | Strong relevance, minor gaps |
| 60-74 | Partial overlap |
| 40-59 | Weak relevance |
| <40 | Not relevant |

---

### 2. Seniority Alignment (20%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Years of experience vs job requirement
- Leadership level (team, national, global)
- Scope (projects vs systems vs policy)
- Stakeholder level (schools vs ministries vs global orgs)
- Scale of responsibility

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 90-100 | Fully aligned or exceeds (P-5-ready) |
| 75-89 | Slightly below but credible |
| 60-74 | Mid-level vs senior mismatch |
| 40-59 | Junior mismatch |
| <40 | Strong mismatch |

**Grade-level reference:**

| Grade | Score Range | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| D-1 / D-2 | 90-95 | Director level, perfect match |
| P-5 | 85-94 | Very strong for 26-year profile |
| P-4 | 75-88 | Strong match |
| P-3 | 60-75 | Mid-level, below target |
| ICS-11 / IICA-3 | 75-85 | UNOPS equivalent to P-4/P-5 |
| IPSA-9 / NPSA-9 | 40-55 | Mid-level service contract |
| Consultant (Senior) | 60-80 | Varies by scope and duration |
| Consultant (Standard) | 40-60 | Below P-level target |
| National Officer (NOB/C) | 25-40 | Below international P-level |
| GS / Field Service | 15-30 | Far below |

---

### 3. Strategic Alignment (20%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Alignment with mission and impact level
- Policy and system transformation experience
- Multi-country or global exposure
- Thought leadership and influence
- Ability to shape strategy and advise stakeholders

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 90-100 | Strong strategic/system-level profile |
| 75-89 | Some strategic exposure |
| 60-74 | Mostly operational |
| 40-59 | Execution-focused |
| <40 | No strategic alignment |

**Role-type reference:**

| Score | Role Type | Examples |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| 90-95 | C-level strategy | Chief/Director of ICT, Presidential Advisor, AI Hub Lead |
| 80-89 | Senior advisory | Digital transformation advisor, policy advisor, programme director |
| 70-79 | Delivery leadership | Project lead, team manager, programme coordinator |
| 60-69 | Technical lead | Senior engineer, architect, specialist |
| 40-55 | Operational | Engineer, analyst, officer (hands-on delivery) |
| <40 | Support | Assistant, associate, administrator |

---

## Advanced Scoring Rules

### Bias Correction
If candidate has non-traditional background (private sector, startup, innovation-driven):
- Do NOT heavily penalize lack of UN/bureaucratic experience
- Give more weight to real-world impact and innovation

### High-Value Signal Boosters
Increase scoring consideration if candidate demonstrates:
- COVID-scale transformation experience
- AI integration in education
- National-level or system-level reform
- Work with governments or ministries
- UNICEF/UN exposure (direct or indirect)

### Red Flags
Decrease scoring if:
- No relevant domain experience
- No leadership for senior roles
- Purely technical with no system understanding
- No stakeholder engagement

|-------------|-------|---------|--------|

## Confidence Level Guidance

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| HIGH | Strong evidence from live page visit, direct experience match, verified details |
| MEDIUM | Good match but some assumptions, or limited information available |
| LOW | Significant uncertainty, domain stretch, or unverified details |

| 40-55% | High — strong match with some advantage |
