# CGIAR Workable Portals for ICT/Director Roles

## Pattern
Several CGIAR research centers use **Workable ATS** (apply.workable.com) for all recruitment, including Director-level ICT/Digital roles. These are not captured by standard UN career portals (INSPIRA) and are inconsistently indexed by Impactpool/UNJobNet.

## Key Example: CIMMYT
- **Careers page:** cimmyt.org/careers/jobs/
- **Workable instance:** apply.workable.com/cimmyt-1/
- **Roles found:** Director IT and Digital Transformation (IRS26239) — Director-level, Internationally Recruited
- **Role pattern:** Business-oriented IT leadership, ERP, Digital Transformation, AI/agents, Knowledge Management, cybersecurity
- **Verification:** Job detail pages render fully via browser_navigate with full description text

## Why Worth Scanning
- Director-level ICT/AI roles often appear here before reaching Impactpool/UNJobNet
- Internationally Recruited (no nationality restrictions)
- CGIAR context = international development, adjacent to UN system
- AI/agent mandates increasingly common in 2026 job descriptions

## Detection Method
1. Check the center's `/careers/jobs/` or `/careers/` page via browser_navigate
2. Extract job titles containing: Director, Chief, Manager, Lead + (IT, Digital, ICT, Technology, Data, AI)
3. Click through to Workable page for full description
4. Verify "Internationally Recruited" or "Locally Recruited" in title
